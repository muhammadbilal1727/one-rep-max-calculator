Privacy Policy

We keep it simple: your numbers stay on your device.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information 1RM Calculator ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the website, you agree to this policy.

Information you enter

The weights, reps, bodyweight and other details you type into the calculator are processed only in your own browser. They are not sent to us, and we do not store them on any server.

Information stored on your device

To make the site more convenient, we save two small preferences in your browser's local storage:

  • Your chosen unit (kg or lbs)
  • Your chosen color theme (light or dark)

These stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-party services we use

Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads a font, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

Hosting

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) in server logs for security and performance.

Analytics and advertising (in the future)

We do not currently use analytics or show ads. However, we may add these in the future to understand how the site is used and to keep it free. If we do:

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site, for example which pages are popular and what devices people use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device, browser and pages visited. This information is used in aggregate and does not directly identify you. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising (such as Google AdSense)

We may show ads from Google or other advertising partners. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings or learn more at aboutads.info.

If we start using these services, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Since we don't collect personal information directly, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you're welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us
